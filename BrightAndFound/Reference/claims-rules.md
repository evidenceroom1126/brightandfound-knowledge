# Claims rules

_The legal load-bearing wall. Checklist item 6 completes this file; the skeleton holds the
frame. Every product page, blog post, email and caption passes this sweep before publish._

## The line

Cosmetic and wellness framing is allowed. Drug framing is forbidden. A product that claims
to treat, cure, prevent or relieve any condition becomes an unapproved drug in FDA terms.

## Forbidden patterns (non-exhaustive; grow this list)

- treats / cures / heals / relieves / reduces anxiety, pain, insomnia, depression, any
  named condition
- anti-inflammatory, antiviral, antibacterial (as benefit claims)
- "clinically proven" anything

## Allowed patterns

- part of your wind-down routine; a calming scent for the evening
- aromas traditionally used in evening rituals
- descriptions of the scent itself, the space, the moment

## Per-SKU proof checklist

- [ ] GC-MS report or COA on file from the supplier
- [ ] Label reviewed (FPLA basics for anything applied to skin)
- [ ] Safety notes written: dilution, pets (cats and dogs especially), pregnancy, children
- [ ] Copy swept against this file
