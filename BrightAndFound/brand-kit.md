# Brand kit

_Seed direction only. It becomes real through comps judged as rendered pixels in a browser
(checklist item 16), never through prose. Use this file for every asset once set._

## Palette (starting direction)

- Base: warm off-white `#FAF7F2`
- Primary: deep sage `#5B6E5A`
- Accent: terracotta `#C8735A`
- Text: charcoal `#2B2B28`

Calm, botanical, premium without being clinical.

## Type (starting direction)

- Display: a warm serif (candidate: Fraunces)
- Body: a humanist sans (candidate: Inter or Source Sans 3)

## Voice

Plain, calm, grounded, sensory. Describes scents, moments and rituals. Never describes
effects on the body (see Reference/claims-rules.md). No em dashes, no en dashes, no
"X, not Y" antithesis constructions, anywhere, including alt text and commit messages.
